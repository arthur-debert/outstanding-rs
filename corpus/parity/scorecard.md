# The parity baseline — gitlike, cargolike and validity against 10.0.0

`gitlike` and `cargolike` are one `corpus-runner batch` invocation's blind
runs; `validity` is the same blind run's binary, its acceptance report
re-evaluated against the corrected `--output term` color-off cases. No
archetype here has an earlier row under this label, so every row's
`Comparable` column reads `single run`.

The objective figures are generated from the committed reports; the reading
under them is authored, and says which of the two it is.

## What was run, and against what

Each run pins standout **10.0.0** from crates.io and provisions the
published documentation snapshot from the checkout at that version
(`docs_source: checkout`). `gitlike` and `cargolike` ran through the
credential broker, blind, once each. `validity`'s binary comes from that
same kind of blind run; only its acceptance suite changed since, so its
committed report is a re-evaluation of the preserved workspace, not a new
session — `evaluation.origin: isolated-re-evaluation` in the report states
this, and its `blindness` block is carried forward unchanged from the
original full run, since re-evaluation reruns only the acceptance and
invariant checks, not the agent phase that block describes.
