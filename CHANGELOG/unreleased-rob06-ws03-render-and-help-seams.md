- Size a whole table from a template (issue #359). `tabular()` and `table()` take a `rows=` argument — the row arrays you are about to render — and resolve every `{"min": n}` / `{"min": n, "max": m}` column against the widest cell those rows hold, which is the measurement a formatter that receives one row at a time cannot make for itself. `table()` measures its `header=` row alongside the data, so a header wider than its column is no longer truncated. Without `rows=` nothing changes: a bounded column still lands on its `min`, and leftover width still goes to the rightmost bounded column when the table has no `"fill"`. A column carrying `sub_columns` is not measured from `rows=` — its sub-columns are resolved per row against the parent's width. See [Introduction to Tabular](docs/crates/render/guides/intro-to-tabular.md), Step 4.
